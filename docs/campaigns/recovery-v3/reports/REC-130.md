# REC-130 — Nested interpretation error paths and consumed positions

## Status

REC-130 is complete. The frozen 48-case matrix passed 48/48 after one
production repair, all six final adjacent suites passed, and the authoritative
Release gate passed all 8/8 targets. The repair preserves nested field paths,
rebases bounded-slice failures to the original substrate, and carries partial
child progress back to the parent cursor.

## Contract coverage

| Family | Cases | Coverage |
|---|---:|---|
| NESTED_POSITION | 12 | Nested schemas, arrays, direct `InterpretAt` offsets and absolute positions |

The contract was verified before observation and again before completion. It
freezes the expected ISE code and byte/character unit for each invalid case;
the permanent fixture additionally asserts the observed cursor position,
qualified field path, parsed prefix and consumed count.

## Initial observations

The first behavioral replay after initial compilation corrections passed 41/48
and retained seven failures. After fixture calibration, r4 passed 43/48 and a
stale-binary replay retained the same five failures. A correctly rebuilt r6
passed 45/48 and isolated SP-05, SP-10 and SP-11 as one production boundary:
`InterpretNestedAt` did not propagate child partial progress or translate the
child error position from the bounded slice to the parent substrate.

Two earlier compile-only attempts (r1 and r3) emitted no TRX/results directory;
their failure class is recorded in the behavior evidence without presenting
compiler output as a behavioral result. The available r2, r4, r5 and r6 TRX
files remain under their unique campaign-artifact directories.

## Finding and repair

REC-130-F01 was confirmed and resolved. A structured substream is interpreted
over a slice beginning at relative offset zero. On a nested `ParseException`,
the parent previously remained at the substream start and the error position
remained slice-relative. `InterpretNestedAt` now captures the parent cursor,
transfers `BytesConsumed - offset` on failure, rebases the error position with
checked arithmetic, and retains the qualified nested field path.

The BINARY-045 regression exposed the same boundary: its one-byte substream
overread now reports original-substrate position 2 instead of bounded-slice
position 1. The regression oracle was updated to the authority-compatible
original-substrate contract and the suite passed 8/8.
