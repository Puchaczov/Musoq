# REC-112 — Callable decision tree and named-argument conflicts

## Outcome

REC-112 completed with a passing 48-case decision-tree matrix. The matrix has
eight six-case families, 25 invalid candidates, and 23 valid ordinary controls.
Every invalid candidate asserts its primary diagnostic code, phase, source
domain, severity, location and message token, together with forbidden-code
absence. The matrix therefore checks the trigger, negative outcome and
deterministic-order behavior for each covered edge.

The final matrix distinguishes callable name, arity, type/overload, owner and
named-source metadata failures. It does not collapse resolution failures into
`MQ3086_UnknownCallable`.

## Contract history

Revision 1 was frozen before observation and remains immutable. During the
first replay, three fixture/oracle details were found to disagree with the
actual language boundary:

- A05 and C04 produce `MQ2034_InvalidNamedSourceArgument` in the parser-owned
  path, so their observed phase is `Parse`, not `Bind`.
- B05's initial `source(1)` seed was invalid against the intentionally
  mismatched string metadata. Revision 2 uses valid positional
  `source('text')` before applying the named-argument mutation.

These corrections are recorded in the oracle-correction artifact. No case was
removed, weakened, or reclassified to hide a failure. The complete revision-2
contract and its newly frozen manifest are the final contract used for
completion.

The invalid cases cover `MQ2034`, `MQ3010`, `MQ3035`, `MQ3079`, `MQ3080`,
`MQ3081`, `MQ3083`, and `MQ3086` through `MQ3089`. The paired forward/reverse
overload cases produce equal diagnostics and candidate messages. The owner
cases retain `MQ3035_AmbiguousMethodOwner` as distinct from
`MQ3089_AmbiguousCallableOverload` and `MQ3083_NamedSourceArgumentsRequireMetadata`.
The canonical-vector controls verify positional-prefix/named-suffix ordering,
hidden `SourceExecutionContext` exclusion, optional default insertion, and
stable delivered values.
