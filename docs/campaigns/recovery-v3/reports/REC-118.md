# REC-118 — Conservative proofs and temporal conversion advisories

## Outcome

REC-118 has a final revision-2 48-case advisory matrix: 23 valid ordinary
controls and 25 valid suspicious cases, with no invalid candidates. The matrix
covers deterministic tautologies, contradictions, floating-point and
null-sensitive proof exclusions, ambiguous numeric date strings, impossible
invariant conversions, and warning-only runtime preservation.

Revision 1 remains immutable. The correction history records the invalid
cross-type dynamic fixture and the authority-backed distinction between the
quiet MQ5003 ambiguity boundary and the specific MQ5025 impossible implicit
conversion diagnostic. Revision 2 preserves all four families and all 48 final
cases.

## Production findings and fixes

- Generic tautology and contradiction proof paths incorrectly treated `IS NULL`
  and `IS NOT NULL` facts as ordinary deterministic identities. The proof paths
  now exclude null-sensitive facts, so nullable predicates do not receive
  MQ5010 or MQ5011 from a proof that exceeds the documented certainty boundary.
- No type or culture coercion was added. Ambiguous numeric date text continues
  to receive MQ5003, while invariant-unparseable implicit constants receive the
  specific MQ5025 impossible-conversion advisory.
