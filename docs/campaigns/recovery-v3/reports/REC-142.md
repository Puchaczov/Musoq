## Scope boundary

This scope audits recovery guidance when source metadata, runtime settings or
provider/runtime facts are unavailable. It verifies that the engine names the
missing observation, preserves Parse/Bind/DataSource boundaries, and offers
safe next steps without promising a unique repair. It validates product CLI
observation routes against the bound command snapshot without executing an
installed CLI. It does not execute reset, reinstall, force, profile, package,
credential or service actions.
