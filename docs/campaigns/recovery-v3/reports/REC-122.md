## Initial finding and repair

The initial frozen replay executed five test methods over the 48 registered
cases. Two methods passed and three failed with eleven assertion failures. The
failures showed that source provenance was lost at several boundaries: parser
nodes lacked exact token spans, clone and rewrite visitors constructed fresh
nodes without copying origins, semantic source binding dropped locations, and
star/result expansion could invent a location. Interpretation sources also
needed a distinct callable-name span so the method token did not become a
plausible but unrelated SQL location.

The bounded production repair captures token-derived spans for CASE, NULL,
IN, CTE and interpretation syntax; copies spans through clone, semantic and
rewrite paths; propagates exact spans through predicate expansion, CTEs,
grouping, result-shape and star expansion; and keeps generated diagnostics
separate from SQL related locations. The ordinality collision diagnostic
continues to use an explicitly unknown location rather than inheriting a
structural APPLY span. No contract or oracle correction was required.
