## Oracle correction and production finding

Revision 1 was frozen before observation and remains preserved. Observation
showed six candidate-construction defects: some output edits were unconsumed,
some nested edits exposed more than the declared root, and H05 carried an
unrelated warning in its valid seed. Revision 2 corrected those fixture
defects while retaining all 48 identifiers and all eight families, then passed
the replay.

The production finding, REC-124-F01, was a coordinate defect in
`Musoq.Parser.Lexer`: `input.Trim()` discarded leading document characters
before token spans were assigned, while `QueryAnalyzer` retained the original
query as `SourceText`. The lexer now retains the exact input; its existing
whitespace-token skipping preserves parsing behavior. The permanent shifted
trajectory and the corrected CTE-coordinate regression both pass.
