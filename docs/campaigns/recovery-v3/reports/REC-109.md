# REC-109 — Misplacement, borrowed dialects and contextual boundaries

## Outcome

REC-109 completed with a three-revision diagnostic matrix. Revision 1 remains
immutable and records a seed-construction failure for the ungrouped `HAVING`
suffix. Revision 2 froze a valid grouped replacement and recorded the observed
parameter-diagnostic oracle and ASOF guidance mismatches. Two independent
read-only campaign reviewers approved the source-justified corrections.

Revision 3 replayed the complete matrix successfully. It contains 29 invalid
candidates and 19 `valid_ordinary` controls across eight recovery families.
Every invalid candidate requires one bounded Parse/Query/Error diagnostic with
the expected phase, source context, explanation, documentation reference and
repair guidance; controls assert successful ordinary parsing where applicable.

## Oracle corrections and finding

Revision 1's `REC-109-M03` seed was not a valid candidate: `HAVING` requires a
preceding grouped-query boundary. Its observation is preserved as a
seed-contract failure, not candidate behavior. Revision 2 uses
`REC-109-M03-v2`, a grouped baseline whose `item group` mutation reaches the
missing APPLY-alias boundary while retaining the `HAVING` suffix.

Revision 2 also established that C#-style `param(string author)` is
`MQ2031_InvalidScriptParameterDeclaration`, not the broader v1 `MQ2032`
expectation. Existing CORE-006 and REWORK-079 contracts provide the independent
authority for `REC-109-D04-v2`.

`REC-109-F01` found that unsupported ASOF RIGHT and ASOF RIGHT OUTER forms
retained `MQ2001` but exposed a false `ASC` keyword repair. The smallest repair
recognizes only the exact ASOF RIGHT phrase, suppresses that misleading repair,
and supplies bounded ASOF-specific alternatives. The repair was validated with
ordinary ASC ordering, quoted text and comments, while the exact Core018 ASOF
RIGHT contract remained intact. No other production diagnostic repair was
required.
