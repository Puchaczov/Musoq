## Scope and disposition

REC-086 reconciles the historical caveats named by the scope against the
current target checkout. It is an inventory and accountability scope. It makes
no production or normative-specification edits, and it does not treat a
generated diagnostic catalog as independent proof of correct guidance.

The machine-readable ledger is
`evidence/REC-086-drift-ledger.json`. It contains 14 stable finding IDs. Every
record has an owner scope, severity, affected version, disposition and
closure-evidence requirements. Open findings are deliberately retained and
assigned to future scopes; the final gate must not conceal them.

## Reconciliation results

Three same-domain documentation conflicts are confirmed in the current
checkout and remain open:

1. `REC-086-CASE-001`: the core specification supports simple CASE (line 78
   and the CASE grammar), while diagnostic catalog entry MQ2026 and the parser
   metadata describe only searched CASE. Owner: REC-089.
2. `REC-086-BINARY-001`: the binary specification says `until` consumes the
   delimiter (lines 1057 and 1070), while its example uses a separate literal
   `': '` immediately after `Key: until ':'` (lines 1060–1063). Owner: REC-140.
3. `REC-086-BINARY-002`: the validation example checks `Data` from `Checksum`
   before `Data` is declared (lines 669–674), while field-reference scoping
   says forward references must not occur (lines 1455–1463). Owner: REC-140.

The other current or historical items are not silently resolved: they are
classified as replay-required, normative-review-required, compatibility-note,
carried-forward, historical-wording, or preserved/unverified dispositions.
The ledger assigns ASOF RIGHT replay to REC-109, typed/dynamic ASOF coverage
to REC-132, diagnostic and wording reconciliation to REC-089, binary/text
reconciliation to REC-140, dynamic-source classification to REC-129, enum
coverage to REC-105, and test-isolation verification to REC-136.

The core passages supporting the reconciliation include the simple-CASE
support statement and grammar, the MQ2026 searched-only metadata, the
case-sensitive function convention versus case-insensitive window-name
guidance, the compatibility-only MQ5009/MQ5020/MQ5026 notes, and the generic
ASOF RIGHT unsupported boundary. Exact source passages and historical links
are stored per finding in the ledger rather than paraphrased as closure.
