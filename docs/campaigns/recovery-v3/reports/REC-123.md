## Oracle correction and initial finding

The revision-1 replay is preserved. Independent review approved revision 2
because G05 originally placed `WHERE` before `FROM`, while G06 selected an
unknown `PartialInterpret` output field. The corrected candidates use valid
source queries and were frozen before replay; the revision-1 contract,
manifest and raw TRX artifacts remain unchanged.

The production finding was a bounded diagnostic-recovery cascade: unresolved
callables could stop independent siblings, while poisoned set outputs,
recursive CTE shapes and aggregate projections could expose derivative errors.
The repair keeps the semantic stack balanced, suppresses only dependent
callable failures, carries poisoned set/recursive outputs through their root,
and retains independent sibling diagnostics.
