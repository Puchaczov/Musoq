# REC-091 — Missing-token repairs, quoting and trivia

REC-091 exercises missing aliases, separators, delimiters and CASE components
at real parser boundaries, together with safe keyword correction and bracketed
identifier controls. The candidate contract requires exact diagnostic spans,
preservation of the next clause, and no guessed TextEdit for missing
expressions, separators or aliases.

## Frozen candidate matrix

The final matrix contains 48 candidate cases and four non-counted valid
bracketed-identifier controls. It is registered in
`evidence/REC-091-pre-observation-manifest.json` and linked by
`evidence/REC-091-case-contract.json`. The matrix covers 12 missing aliases,
10 missing separators, 10 missing closing delimiters, 9 mandatory CASE
components, and 7 misspelled keywords with safe emitted quick-fixes. It also
contains EOF, comments, CRLF and multiline-trivia boundaries.

An initial observed iteration ran before the final replay freeze and reported
33 passed and 15 failed. That feedback is explicitly excluded from candidate
evidence. It exposed two unsafe keyword edits, corrected parser insertion-span
expectations to the documented token ends, and identified that the initial K06
candidate omitted the grammar-required GROUP BY. K06 was revised to remain a
one-fault HAVING typo case; the final matrix revision was frozen before the
final replay.

The seven registered word-like keyword fixes were applied from the returned
diagnostic TextEdit, compared with their exact repaired query, and reparsed
without diagnostics. Missing-token and alias cases emitted no unsafe edit.
The production guard in
`src/dotnet/Musoq.Parser/Diagnostics/SyntaxDiagnosticEnhancer.KeywordSuggestions.cs`
prevents non-word pseudo-keywords and field labels immediately before `:` from
receiving a keyword TextEdit. This eliminates the observed structural-brace
to-`BY` and `Age`-to-`ASC` false fixes without changing the safe keyword cases.

The 36 registered boundary assertions show that diagnostics do not consume the
next clause token. The four bracketed controls reparse reserved names and
space-containing names, preserving the selected lexical contract.

Focused final replay: 52 passed, 0 skipped, 0 failed.
