## Finding and repair

REC-126-F01 is a confirmed production defect. When a source-contract
diagnostic had no modifier or column mapping,
SourceContractDiagnosticReporter.ResolveSpan returned TextSpan.Empty.
DiagnosticContext then mapped that span through the query SourceText,
fabricating a known offset-zero location.

The repair makes source-contract span resolution nullable. Missing mappings use
the no-location reporting overloads and remain SourceLocation.None; mapped
modifier or column spans retain their exact coordinates. The direct
DiagnosticContext.ReportWarning(..., TextSpan.Empty) API remains unchanged
and is covered by D01 as the known-insertion control.
