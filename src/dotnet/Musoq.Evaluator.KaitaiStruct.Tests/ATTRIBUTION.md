from [kaitai-io/kaitai_struct_tests](https://github.com/kaitai-io/kaitai_struct_tests),
pinned at `59afee013e1a8e5fb894ca99838f55ef7b329cb3`.

cover Kaitai's separate real-world format or sample galleries.
